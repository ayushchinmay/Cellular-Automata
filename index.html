RGB Life

Conway's Game of Life played on the pixels of your photos. Three or six species compete on one board under nine cross-species interaction models — predation, mutualism, mimicry, hybrid births. Load an image, paint cells, stamp verified patterns, and watch a picture collapse into gliders.
